Private by design

CARRERAS ANTOINE stores records locally on the device. The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment details, or health data.

  • No account registration
  • No cloud sync
  • No Android permissions requested
  • Records can be cleared from the app settings
